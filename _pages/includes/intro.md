I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at [The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHK-Shenzhen)](https://www.cuhk.edu.cn/en), advised by Professor [Zhizheng Wu](http://www.drwuz.com/) and Professor [Haizhou Li](https://www.colips.org/~eleliha/).

My research focuses on trustworthy speech and audio intelligence, especially **audio deepfake detection**, **spoken misinformation detection**, **speaker verification security**, and **audio language models**. I am interested in building reliable AI systems that can detect, explain, and defend against synthetic or adversarial audio in realistic scenarios.

Before joining CUHK-Shenzhen, I received my master's degree from [Peking University](https://english.pku.edu.cn/) (北京大学), where I worked on spoken keyword spotting and speaker verification with Professor [Yuexian Zou (邹月娴)](https://web.pkusz.edu.cn/adsp/). I received my bachelor's degree from [University of Electronic Science and Technology of China](https://www.uestc.edu.cn/) (电子科技大学), majoring in Software Engineering.

My work has appeared in IEEE/ACM TASLP, ICLR, IEEE SLT, ICASSP, and Interspeech. My recent first-author paper **GenTraceBench** was accepted by IEEE ISCSLP 2026, and my first-author manuscript **RealComm** was submitted to IEEE/ACM TASLP. I have also worked on industry-facing speech and audio projects with Huawei, ByteDance, Tencent AI Lab, Xiaomi, Shenzhen Research Institute of Big Data, and International Digital Economy Academy.
